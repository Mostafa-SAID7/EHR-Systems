## Quick Facts

**Company:** TachyHealth  
**Headquarters:** Dubai, UAE  
**Focus:** Medical coding automation + healthcare AI  
**Stage:** Series A (raised $5M from Al-Tawuniya)  
**Market:** MENA healthcare (emerging market)  
**Problem:** Medical coding is manual, expensive, error-prone  
**Solution:** AI-powered medical coding automation

## Why They Matter

- **Real Problem:** Hospitals struggling with medical coding
- **Real Validation:** Series A with institutional backing (not venture hype)
- **Real Market:** MENA healthcare underserved + growing
- **Real Opportunity:** First-mover advantage in emerging market

## Key Points to Remember

1. **What They Do**
   - Automate medical coding process
   - Transform visit notes → ICD-10/CPT codes
   - Help hospitals optimize revenue cycle
   - AI/ML-powered suggestion system

2. **Who They Serve**
   - Hospitals and healthcare providers
   - Primarily MENA region
   - Different from US (different regulations, different needs)

3. **Why Series A Matters**
   - Product-market fit validated
   - Customers actually using and paying
   - Growth is real, not speculative

4. **Why MENA Matters**
   - Huge healthcare transformation opportunity
   - Digital healthcare adoption accelerating
   - Underserved vs. saturated US market
   - Government support for healthcare innovation

## Interview Talking Points

**When they ask "What do you know about TachyHealth?"**

"TachyHealth is solving a real healthcare problem I've seen - medical coding is expensive and error-prone. You're automating it with AI, which directly improves hospital operations and revenue.

What's most interesting is MENA focus. Healthcare digitization in emerging markets is still early; that's where the impact is. Series A validation from Al-Tawuniya shows this isn't hype - real hospitals trust your system.

The technical challenge is compelling too - medical coding accuracy matters, compliance requirements are strict, scale is significant. That's genuinely interesting engineering."
