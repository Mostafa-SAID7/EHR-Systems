# TachyHealth Market Research & Domain Context

Deep dive into the MENA healthcare automation landscape and TachyHealth's market positioning.

---

## 🏥 Market Problem: Medical Coding Bottleneck

1. **Manual Labor Costs**: Medical coding (ICD-10-CM / CPT / ACHI) requires trained specialists manually reviewing doctor notes.
2. **Revenue Cycle Delays**: Coding backlogs slow down claims submission, leading to 30-90 day payment cycles for hospitals.
3. **High Rejection Rates**: Errors in manual coding lead to claim denials by insurance companies (e.g., Tawuniya, NPHIES integration in KSA/UAE).
4. **Regulatory Expansion**: Mandatory e-health initiatives across Saudi Arabia (NPHIES) and UAE (DHA/Nabidh) require digital precision.

---

## 🚀 TachyHealth Solution & Technology Stack

- **AI-Powered Code Suggestions**: NLP models scan unstructured clinical notes and map them to standard billing codes.
- **Coder Workbench**: Semi-automated human-in-the-loop workflow where coders verify AI suggestions.
- **Audit & Compliance Engine**: Immutable logging to satisfy regulatory audits and prevent fraudulent billing claims.

---

## 📈 Strategic Advantage

- **MENA First-Mover**: Optimized for regional clinical workflows (bilingual Arabic/English documentation patterns).
- **Institutional Validation**: $5M Series A funding led by Al-Tawuniya (Saudi Arabia's largest insurance provider).
