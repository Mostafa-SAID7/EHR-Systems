# TachyHealth Technical & Product Strategy

---

## 🗺️ Engineering Growth Horizon

1. **Phase 1: Core Coding Assistant**:
   - High accuracy ICD-10/CPT suggestion engine with fast response latency (< 500ms).
2. **Phase 2: Full Revenue Cycle Automation**:
   - Direct integration with EHRs (FHIR / HL7 standards) and NPHIES insurance claim portals.
3. **Phase 3: Autonomous Pre-Authorization**:
   - Real-time approval prediction before procedures are performed.

---

## 🏗️ Architectural Requirements for Engineering Hiring

- **Scale & Reliability**: High availability microservices handling peak hospital discharge hours.
- **Auditability**: Complete trace of AI suggestion confidence scores vs coder manual overrides.
- **Security**: Zero data leak tolerance for Protected Health Information (PHI).
