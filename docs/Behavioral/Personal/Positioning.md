## 🗣️ Verbal Scripts

### 1. "Tell me about yourself"

```text
"Sure! I'm a Senior Full Stack Developer. Over the last 3 years at We3ds, I've been building a multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace from the ground up. In that role, I solved complex distributed system challenges—things like real-time inventory locking across 100+ concurrent buyers, multi-vendor fee reconciliation, and event-driven transaction processing using Kafka and Redis.

Prior to and alongside my e-commerce work, I spent significant time building healthcare systems, specifically EHR platforms with HIPAA-compliant audit trails, ICD-10/CPT coding workflows, and microservices architectures.

I'm looking to join TachyHealth because medical coding automation is the perfect synthesis of my background: it requires high-reliability system design, handling real-time workflows, and a deep respect for healthcare domain accuracy."
```

### 2. "Why leave e-commerce for TachyHealth?"

```text
"E-commerce was fantastic for teaching me scale, peak load handling, and real-time consistency under pressure. However, healthcare is a domain where technical correctness directly impacts clinical efficiency and hospital revenue. 

TachyHealth stands out to me because:
1. It solves a real, painful problem in medical coding automation.
2. It has Series A validation and strategic institutional backing from Al-Tawuniya.
3. The MENA regional focus presents a massive first-mover advantage that I'm genuinely excited to contribute to."
```
