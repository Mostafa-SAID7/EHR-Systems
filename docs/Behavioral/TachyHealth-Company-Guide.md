### What They Do
TachyHealth is a **healthcare AI startup** specializing in:
- **AI-Powered Medical Coding** - Automates medical coding from patient records
- **Revenue Cycle Management (RCM)** - Streamlines billing and claims processing
- **Decision Support Systems** - Guides doctors through patient encounters
- **Healthcare Fraud Detection** - Uses AI to identify billing fraud
- **Value-Based Healthcare Solutions** - For both payers (insurance) and providers (hospitals)
- **Claims Processing Automation** - Reduces denial rates, speeds reimbursement
- **AiPharma** - Pharmaceutical insights for better prescribing decisions
