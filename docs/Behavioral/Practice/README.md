## Interview Types

### Behavioral Round (45-60 min)

**What they're assessing:**
- Communication (can you explain yourself?)
- Problem-solving (how do you approach challenges?)
- Teamwork (do you work well with others?)
- Learning (can you grow?)
- Alignment (do you fit our culture?)

**What to prepare:**
- 5 STAR stories (see Stories folder)
- Answers to common behavioral questions
- Questions to ask them

**Example Questions:**
- "Tell me about yourself"
- "Tell me about a challenge you faced"
- "Tell me about a time you failed"
- "Tell me about working with others"
- "Why TachyHealth?"
- "What's your biggest weakness?"

### Technical Round (45-90 min)

**What they're assessing:**
- Technical depth (do you know your stuff?)
- System design (architectural thinking)
- Problem-solving (how do you approach problems?)
- Communication (can you explain technical concepts?)
- Collaboration (do you ask clarifying questions?)

**What to prepare:**
- System design walkthrough (medical coding)
- Patterns (CQRS, Events, Saga, Outbox)
- Your real-world examples
- Clarifying questions

**Example Questions:**
- "Design a medical coding system"
- "How would you handle 10x growth?"
- "What happens when this fails?"
- "Tell me about your microservices experience"
- "How do you approach database optimization?"

### Combination Round (60-90 min)

**Mixed behavioral + technical**

**What to prepare:**
- All of above
- Be ready to shift between storytelling and technical design
- Connect your stories to technical decisions

## Interview Preparation Timeline

### 1 Week Before

**Monday-Wednesday:**
- [ ] Read Company/Overview.md (know who they are)
- [ ] Read Personal/Profile.md (know your story)
- [ ] Read Stories/README.md (overview your 5 stories)

**Thursday-Friday:**
- [ ] Read Questions-Answers.md (common Q&A)
- [ ] Read Technical/README.md (system design overview)
- [ ] Create your list of questions for them

### 3 Days Before

**Day 1:**
- [ ] Practice telling each story out loud (5x each)
- [ ] Time yourself (target: 5 min per story)
- [ ] Record yourself (listen back, note improvements)

**Day 2:**
- [ ] Read Technical/System-Design.md (deep dive)
- [ ] Read Technical/Patterns.md (understand patterns)
- [ ] Practice system design conversation

**Day 3:**
- [ ] Mock interview with colleague (or self)
- [ ] Run through questions
- [ ] Get feedback on delivery

### 1 Day Before

**Afternoon:**
- [ ] Tell all 5 stories once (confidence check)
- [ ] Read Mock-Interview.md (see flow)
- [ ] Review your questions

**Evening:**
- [ ] Early sleep (good rest matters)
- [ ] Don't cram (reviewing > learning new)
- [ ] Relax (you're prepared)

### Morning Of

**1 hour before:**
- [ ] Read Checklist.md (final checklist)
- [ ] Tell stories once (confidence)
- [ ] Review 3 talking points

**30 min before:**
- [ ] Read Tips.md (techniques)
- [ ] Deep breath
- [ ] Remember: They want this to work out too

**15 min before:**
- [ ] Water (stay hydrated)
- [ ] Smile (even for video call, affects tone)
- [ ] Remember your preparation

## Quick Reference - Remember During Interview

**Behavioral:**
✅ Tell complete stories (S-T-A-R)  
✅ Give specific details (makes stories real)  
✅ Show your thinking (explain why)  
✅ Mention learning (every story should have learning)  
✅ Ask questions (keeps conversation going)  

**Technical:**
✅ Ask clarifying questions (requirements? scale?)  
✅ Start high-level (big picture first)  
✅ Think out loud (show your thinking)  
✅ Explain tradeoffs (why this not that?)  
✅ Mention operations (monitoring, reliability)  

**General:**
✅ Be authentic (don't pretend to know what you don't)  
✅ Be interested (ask good questions)  
✅ Be respectful (listen carefully)  
✅ Be confident (you know your stuff)
