# Interview Tips & Delivery Best Practices

---

## 💡 Key Delivery Tips

1. **Structure Answers with STAR**: Situation → Task → Action → Result.
2. **Be Specific with Metrics**: Mention 100+ concurrent inventory users, sub-500ms latency, and Series A market opportunities.
3. **Show Domain Curiosity**: Connect high-scale e-commerce architecture to healthcare data accuracy and compliance.
