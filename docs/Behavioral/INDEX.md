## ⚡ Quick Tips

### During Interview

**If asked something you don't know:**
- Don't make it up
- "That's a good question. My initial thinking would be... 
  How does that align with your current approach?"
- Shows intellectual honesty + curiosity

**If asked to design something:**
- ASK CLARIFYING QUESTIONS FIRST
- "Help me understand the constraints: scale, latency requirements, 
  existing infrastructure?"
- Shows you think like architect

**If nervous:**
- Take deep breath
- Remember: They want to hire you
- You know more than you think
- It's okay to pause and think

**If story is taking too long:**
- Shorten it
- Jump to key point
- "Let me skip to the result..."
