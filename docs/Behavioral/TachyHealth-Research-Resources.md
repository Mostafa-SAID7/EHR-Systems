## 🔍 Deep Dive Topics (Optional)

### If You Want Extra Preparation

#### Topic 1: Medical Coding Systems
```
ICD-10: Diagnosis codes (what the patient has)
- Example: E11.9 = Type 2 diabetes without complications

CPT: Procedure codes (what the doctor did)
- Example: 99213 = Office visit, established patient, low complexity

DRG: Diagnosis Related Groups (payment groups)
- Bundles diagnosis + procedures into payment amount

Why it matters: Incorrect coding = claim denial = lost revenue
```

#### Topic 2: Revenue Cycle Problem
```
Timeline: Patient visit to payment can take 60-90 days
- Day 1: Patient visit, clinical documentation
- Days 1-5: Manual medical coding (SLOW)
- Days 6-10: Claim submission
- Days 11-40: Insurance processing
- Days 41-90: Payment/denial handling

Friction: Every manual step adds delay + opportunity for error

TachyHealth Impact: Automate step 2 (manual coding) from days to minutes
```

#### Topic 3: Fraud Detection Opportunity
```
Healthcare fraud costs ~$68B annually in US
MENA estimated similar percentage of lower healthcare spend = billions

Common fraud patterns:
- Billing for procedures never done
- Upcoding (billing higher complexity than actual)
- Duplicate billing
- Unbundling (splitting one procedure into many)

Why hard to detect: Thousands of claims daily, complex patterns

TachyHealth Solution: ML detects anomalies humans miss
```
