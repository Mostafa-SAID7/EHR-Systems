# TachyHealth - Quick Reference for Interviews

## ⚡ 60-Second Company Summary

**TachyHealth** is a Series A-funded healthcare AI startup (Dubai-based) that automates medical coding and revenue cycle management for hospitals, insurance companies, and health systems across the Middle East & Africa. 

**Founded:** 2018 | **Funding:** $7.66M total ($5M Series A from Al-Tawuniya Insurance) | **Status:** Generating revenue, scaling

**What They Do:** AI reads patient records (even handwritten) → automatically generates medical codes → reduces billing denials from 18% to 6-8% → faster payment for hospitals/providers

## 🏥 What They Solve

### Problem 1: Manual Medical Coding
- **Current:** Doctors' notes → Manual coding by humans → 2-8 hours per patient
- **TachyHealth:** Any document (handwritten/digital) → AI → Medical codes → Minutes

### Problem 2: Healthcare Fraud
- **Current:** Billions in fraudulent claims hard to detect
- **TachyHealth:** ML detects suspicious billing patterns → Prevents fraud

### Problem 3: Revenue Cycle Delays
- **Current:** Patient visit to payment: 60-90 days, error-prone
- **TachyHealth:** Automates entire cycle → Faster payment

### Impact
- **Denial rates:** Reduced from 18% to 6-8%
- **Processing time:** Hours to minutes
- **Accuracy:** Higher than manual coding

## 🎓 Healthcare Domain Context

### Medical Coding (What They Automate)
- ICD-10: Diagnosis codes (what patient has)
- CPT: Procedure codes (what doctor did)
- Important because: Wrong codes = claim denial = lost revenue

### Revenue Cycle (What They Solve)
Flow: Patient Visit → Documentation → **Coding [SLOW/ERROR-PRONE]** → Claim Submission → Insurance Processing → Payment

TachyHealth speeds up the **Coding** step massively

### Value-Based Healthcare (Why It Matters)
- Insurance companies increasingly pay for outcomes, not services
- Drives demand for efficiency automation
- Accurate data (proper coding) = accurate analytics
